The gap

Psychiatric disorders still rely on subjective symptom checklists. Delays in accurate diagnosis can last years. We aim to shorten that window with objective neural markers.

Our approach

High‑density resting EEG combined with robust machine learning. We identify disorder‑specific signatures across MDD, schizophrenia, bipolar, anxiety, OCD, PTSD.

Open & reproducible

We pre‑register analyses, share pretrained models, and collaborate with academic hospitals. Our dream: accessible, low‑cost EEG screening.

Ethics first

IRB‑approved, GDPR & HIPAA compliant. We prioritize patient privacy and algorithmic fairness across age, gender, and ethnicity.

Built with scientific rigor

  • 64‑channel EEG, 5 min resting state
  • Automated artifact rejection (ICA + wavelet)
  • Spectral + connectivity + microstate features
  • Gradient boosting, attention CNN, ensemble
  • SHAP & LIME for biomarker localization
  • Multi‑site domain generalisation
